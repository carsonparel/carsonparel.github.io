Spring-Loaded Ball Launcher

UC San Diego | Oct – Dec 2025

Project Overview

Designed and fabricated a competition robot with a motor-driven rack-and-sector-gear launcher and a four-bar linkage for repeatable ball positioning.

My Contributions

Led a team of four through design and fabrication using 3D printing and laser cutting. Replaced a motorized claw with a passive rubber-band gripper to reduce motor channels from five to four.

Engineering Analysis and Results

Modeled elastic energy storage of 0.108 J and inelastic rod-ball impact. Predicted a ball speed of 1.02 m/s compared with a measured 0.9 m/s. Analysis identified pullback distance rather than motor torque as the limiting factor.

Flow Control and Coordinated Robotics (FCCR) Lab

Undergraduate Research · UC San Diego

Research Overview

Our team is developing a quadcopter research platform to execute periodic flight trajectories for probabilistic search.

Current Development

The team is investigating uneven motor response and flight instability, integrating a replacement flight controller, and developing mounting solutions for the power distribution board.

Testing and Integration

Bench testing in Betaflight has included individual motor response checks, receiver calibration, and sensor checks. The cause of the observed flight instability has not yet been confirmed.

Trajectory Implementation

The research objective is to translate periodic search trajectories into autonomous flight and eventually compare commanded and measured trajectories.
